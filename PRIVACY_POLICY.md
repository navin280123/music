# Privacy Policy for Pocketo Play

**Last updated:** August 2025  
**App Name:** Pocketo Play  
**Package Name:** `com.navnit.pocketoplay`

---

## 1. Overview
Pocketo Play is an offline music player designed to play audio files stored locally on your Android device. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
**We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.**
- No account registration or login is required.
- No personal identification information (such as name, email address, or phone number) is collected.
- No device identifiers or location information are collected.
- No audio files or media data leave your device.

## 3. Permissions Used
The app requests standard permissions strictly necessary for core music playback functionality:
- **`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`** (Android 13+): Used to discover and play local music files on your device.
- **`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`** (Android 12 & below): Used to discover and play local music files on your device.
- **`FOREGROUND_SERVICE` & `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`**: Used to continue audio playback when the app is in the background or screen is turned off.
- **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`**: Used to display playback controls in your notification panel.
- **`WAKE_LOCK`**: Keeps CPU awake during audio playback to prevent stutter or stops.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Analytics
Pocketo Play does **NOT** integrate third-party tracking, advertising networks, or analytics tools.

## 5. Children’s Privacy
Our application does not collect any personal data and is safe for users of all ages.
