01 / Overview

About Pocketo Play

Pocketo Play (Application ID: com.navnit.pocketoplay) is a standalone, offline music player application designed to browse and play audio files stored locally on your device.

We respect your privacy. This policy outlines how information and permissions are handled.

02 / Data Collection & Usage

Information Collection & Storage

We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal or identifiable information.

  • No personal identifiable information (name, email, phone)
  • No device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • No location or browsing data
  • No audio content or files uploaded to external servers

All app settings, favorite songs, and playlists are stored locally on your device using Android local storage and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

03 / Device Permissions

Permissions Used by the App

Permission Purpose
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Required on Android 13+ to scan and play local music files.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Required on Android 12 and below to access local audio files.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Allows uninterrupted music playback while using other apps or when screen is off.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays media playback controls (play, pause, next) in the notification bar.
WAKE_LOCK Prevents playback interruption during screen sleep.
Audio permissions are used solely to read and play audio tracks on the device. No audio or media files are ever transferred or uploaded.
04 / Third-Party Services

Third-Party SDKs & Analytics

Pocketo Play does NOT use any third-party analytics (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics), advertising networks, or tracking services.

Open-source Flutter libraries used by the app operate strictly locally on device for audio playback, state management, and media notification controls.

Children's Privacy
